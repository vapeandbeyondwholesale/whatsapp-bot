Privacy Policy

Vape & Beyond Wholesale ("we", "us") operates a WhatsApp-based customer service assistant to help customers with product, pricing, ordering, and delivery questions. This policy explains what information we collect through that service and how it is used.

Information We Collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and the messages you send us, so we can respond to your questions.
  • Basic conversation history, kept temporarily to maintain context during a conversation.

How We Use Your Information

  • To answer your questions about products, pricing, delivery, and orders.
  • To look up product and pricing information from our store to share with you.

Third-Party Services

To provide this service, your messages are processed by:

  • Meta / WhatsApp Business Platform - to send and receive WhatsApp messages.
  • OpenAI - to generate helpful responses to your questions.
  • Shopify - to look up our product catalog and pricing.

We do not sell or share your information with any other third party for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

Conversation history is kept only for a short period to keep the conversation contextual, and is automatically deleted after a period of inactivity.

Your Choices

You can stop the conversation at any time by simply not replying. To request deletion of your data, contact us using the details below.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, please contact us at vapeandbeyondwholesale@gmail.com.
